Ancestral Memory & Chronicle

History & Heritage of Eruwaland

A structured presentation of the founding tradition, royal lineage and cultural memory contained in the supplied source website.

Princess Oyinlola & Obaseeku

According to the supplied Eruwa oral tradition, Princess Oyinlola, daughter of the Alaafin of Oyo, encountered Obaseeku, a hunter and nobleman. Their union is presented as a foundational episode in the story of Eruwaland.

Olaribikusi & the Crown

The supplied account states that Obaseeku returned to Oyo with young Olaribikusi, who became fascinated by the royal crown. The Alaafin's act of placing the crown in his hands is remembered as part of the origin story of the Eleruwa stool.

The Meaning of Eruwa

The source connects the name Eruwa with the expression “Eriwanibio”, associated with the sight or availability of roasted yam along old travel routes and with the hospitality of the people.

Living Heritage

Heritage on this portal should be treated as living cultural memory: oral tradition, royal institutions, language, festivals, places, regalia and community knowledge.

Chronicle of Kings

The Eleruwa Lineage

Early tradition

Olaribikusi

Presented in the supplied source as the first crowned Eleruwa and son of Obaseeku and Princess Oyinlola.

Royal succession

Olasubu Akangbe Osin

Remembered in the supplied account as a ruler associated with wisdom and peaceful leadership.

Royal houses

Gbajumola I, Sabi Ayinla Edu, Olurin Iyanda Agan & Omoni Alade Efon

The source groups these reigns within the older development of the Eruwa community around its valleys and hills.

Modern era

Sangotola I, Bankole Agan, Ajao Oti, Bameke Agan, Akinyele & Adegboye

The supplied chronology identifies these reigns as part of later institutional consolidation.

1998–present

Oba Samuel Adebayo Adegbola Ajobo Olurin I

The current reign is presented as a continuation of the Eleruwa institution and a return to the throne in 2025.
