The Sovereign of Eruwaland

HRM Oba Samuel Adebayo Adegbola

Ajobo Olurin I — The Eleruwa of Eruwaland. A royal biography and institutional profile.

Royal Heritage & Biography

His Royal Majesty brings together significant royal lineages in Eruwaland. The source material supplied with this website traces his paternal ancestry through the Olurin royal family of the Akalako Royal House and his maternal heritage through the Ajao royal family of the Olaribikusi Ruling House.

He commenced formal education at Islamic Primary School, Sango, Eruwa, later attended Obaseku High School, and proceeded to Yaba College of Technology, where the source records an Ordinary National Diploma in Business Administration and Management in 1984.

He joined the Nigeria Customs Service in 1976 and served in major operational locations before retiring in 1998 as a Superintendent of Customs.

Royal Milestones

  • Born: 15 January 1952 — Olurin Compound, Oke Oba.
  • First installed: 16 July 1998.
  • First reign: 1998–2019.
  • Selected again in 2024 following a process involving 18 candidates from the Olurin family, according to the supplied source.
  • Appointed / presented with instruments of office: 6 March 2025.
  • Coronation: 28 June 2025.
