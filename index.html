The Throne Spotlight

A Living Institution of Heritage, Dignity and Service

His Royal Majesty, Oba Samuel Adebayo Adegbola Ajobo Olurin I, stands at the centre of a royal institution whose story is deeply connected to Eruwaland's ancestral memory, traditional governance and communal identity.

The uploaded source design records his birth on 15 January 1952 at Olurin Compound, Oke Oba; his education at Yaba College of Technology; his career in the Nigeria Customs Service; his first reign from 1998 to 2019; and his return to the throne following his appointment in March 2025.
