The Kingdom & Tourism

A gateway for visitors, researchers, the diaspora and cultural partners seeking to understand Eruwaland.

A Kingdom of Living Heritage

Eruwaland's heritage combines royal institutions, oral history, community identity, hills and historic places. A future Palace tourism section can provide verified visitor information, cultural protocols, heritage trails, accommodation guidance and official event calendars.

Tourism content should be developed with local historians, community custodians and relevant authorities so that sacred or sensitive heritage is represented respectfully.
