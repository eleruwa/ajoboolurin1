Historic Landscape

Principal Hills of Eruwaland

Ilewu Hill

The supplied source locates Ilewu behind Aafin Eleruwa and Olurin Compound, Oke-Oba, and describes its connection with early settlement and views over Eruwa.

Okele / Obaseeku Hill

The source associates the hill near L.A. School, Isale-Kola, with Obaseeku and identifies his burial place at its foot.

Apanpa Hill

The supplied website describes Apanpa as a strategically significant place of refuge for early settlers.

Onigbaporo

Presented in the source as a revered historic place of refuge during periods of conflict and migration.

Akolu Hill

The supplied source places Akolu along the Eruwa–Igboora Road at Aborerin and describes its historical defensive significance.

Ojoko Hill

The source associates Ojoko, near Ilupeju Baptist Church, Sango, with an early stage of the settlement's movement.
